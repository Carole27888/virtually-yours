A standard operating procedure (SOP) is a written guide to doing a task the same way every time. Good SOPs make delegation faster, reduce mistakes and mean your business does not rely on memory.

## Keep it to one task

An SOP should cover one clear task, such as replying to a new inquiry or publishing a blog post. If it covers several, split it up.

## Use a simple structure

1. Purpose: why this task matters
2. When: what triggers the task and how often
3. Tools: links and logins needed
4. Steps: numbered, one action per step
5. Quality check: what done looks like

## Show, do not just tell

A short screen recording alongside written steps makes an SOP much easier to follow, especially for tasks with many clicks.

> If a new team member cannot complete the task from your SOP alone, the SOP needs another pass.

## Review it regularly

Tools and preferences change. Ask your assistant to flag any step that no longer matches reality and update the SOP as you go.
