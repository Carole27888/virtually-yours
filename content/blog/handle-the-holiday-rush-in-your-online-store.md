For online store owners, the weeks from Black Friday to the new year can be the busiest of the year. Orders increase, customer questions multiply and every delay feels bigger because shoppers want their gifts on time. It is exciting, and it can also be exhausting.

The stores that come through the season well are not always the biggest. They are the ones that prepared, kept their systems simple and made sure no single person was trying to do everything. Here is how to handle the rush without burning out.

## Get your customer service ready

During busy periods, many of the questions you receive are the same. Where is my order? When will it arrive? Can I change my address? How do returns work?

Prepare for them:

- **Write saved replies** for your ten most common questions
- **Update your FAQ page** with shipping deadlines, holiday hours and return policies
- **Add clear delivery cut-off dates** to your product pages and checkout
- **Set expectations for response times**, for example replying within one business day

When the answers are ready, each customer message takes a fraction of the time to handle.

## Keep customers informed about their orders

Many customer questions disappear when people know what is happening with their order. Check that your store sends clear emails for:

- Order confirmation
- Shipping confirmation with tracking
- Delays, if they happen

If there is a delay with a supplier or courier, tell affected customers before they ask. A short, honest update builds trust, even when the news is not ideal.

## Check your stock and listings

Running out of a popular item, or selling something you no longer have, causes stress for you and disappointment for customers. Before the rush:

- Check stock levels on your best sellers
- Hide or mark products that will not arrive in time
- Make sure prices and discounts are set up correctly
- Test your discount codes and checkout process

A quick test order can catch problems before your customers do.

## Plan your marketing in advance

The holiday season is not the time to be writing emails and social media posts at midnight. Plan and schedule your key messages ahead of time:

- Your Black Friday and Cyber Monday offers
- Gift guides for different types of customers
- Last order dates for delivery before the holidays
- Post-holiday offers and new year messages

Once everything is scheduled, you can focus on serving customers rather than thinking about what to post next.

## Watch the small things that cause big problems

During busy periods, small issues can quickly grow. Keep an eye on:

- **Failed payments** and abandoned checkouts, which may need a friendly follow-up
- **Address errors**, which are easier to fix before an order ships
- **Negative reviews or comments**, which deserve a calm, quick response
- **Low stock alerts**, so you can update listings quickly

A daily check of these takes a few minutes and can save hours later.

## Make returns simple

The season does not end when the presents are opened. Returns and exchanges often increase in January. Make your returns process clear and easy, and prepare saved replies for return requests. A smooth return experience can turn a disappointed customer into a loyal one.

## Get help before you need it

The biggest mistake many store owners make is waiting until they are overwhelmed before getting help. By then, there is no time to explain anything.

An assistant with e-commerce experience can:

- Answer customer messages using your saved replies
- Track orders and update customers about delays
- Process returns and exchanges
- Update product listings, stock and prices
- Schedule marketing emails and social media posts
- Send you a daily summary of anything that needs your attention

Bringing someone in before or early in the season gives them time to learn your store while things are still manageable.

## Look after yourself

It is easy to run on adrenaline through December and then crash in January. A few habits help:

- Set working hours, even during busy weeks, and stick to them as much as possible
- Take short breaks away from screens
- Decide in advance which days you will fully switch off
- Celebrate the wins along the way

Your store depends on you being well. A tired, stressed owner makes more mistakes and enjoys the business less.

## A holiday season checklist

- Saved replies for your top questions
- FAQ updated with shipping deadlines and holiday hours
- Delivery cut-off dates on product pages
- Order and shipping emails checked
- Stock checked and listings updated
- Discount codes tested
- Marketing planned and scheduled
- Daily check of payments, addresses, reviews and stock
- Simple returns process ready
- Support in place before the busiest weeks

## Your next step

Pick three items from the checklist and complete them today. Even a little preparation makes the busiest weeks noticeably smoother.
