HOW TO USE THIS TEMPLATE (delete these notes before publishing)
1. Copy this file and rename the copy, using lowercase words and hyphens.
   The file name becomes the web address:
   how-to-delegate-email.md  ->  /blog/how-to-delegate-email
2. Fill in the details between the --- lines at the top:
   - date: the day the post goes live (YYYY-MM-DD). A future date
     schedules the post. It stays hidden until that day.
   - category: keep to a few, for example Delegation, Systems,
     Productivity, Hiring, Growth.
   - draft: true hides the post. Delete that line when it is ready.
3. Length: aim for 1,000 to 1,500 words. Posts under 600 words get a
   warning when the site builds, because short pages rarely rank.
4. Cover photo (optional but recommended): save a landscape photo,
   about 1600 x 840 pixels, in public/images/blog/ and a smaller copy
   (about 800 x 420) ending in -sm.jpg. Only use photos you have the
   right to use, such as your own or free photos from unsplash.com.
   If you leave the image lines out, the post simply has no photo.
   imageSmall is optional; without it the cards use the large photo.
5. Google shows about 60 characters of a title. If your title plus
   " | Virtually Handled Co." is longer, the brand is dropped. If the
   title alone is over 60, add a shorter seoTitle line. The build
   warns you about titles and excerpts that are too long.
6. Files starting with _ (like this one) are never published.
